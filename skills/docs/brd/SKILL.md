# Business Requirements Document (BRD) Skill

Standardized engineering skill for creating, updating, and verifying **BRD** documentation conforming to Layer 15 Universal Project Documentation Standard.

## When to Use

* Justifying new engineering initiatives to business stakeholders.
* Defining measurable commercial KPIs, cost reduction goals, and onboarding speeds.
* Analyzing multi-stakeholder needs (customers, operators, compliance, leadership).
* Setting financial budgets and licensing boundaries.

---

## 🎯 Core Capabilities

* **Capability 1:** Structures commercial objectives into quantifiable disruption metrics.
* **Capability 2:** Builds comprehensive KPI scorecards with baseline vs target metrics.
* **Capability 3:** Performs stakeholder impact analysis across user segments.
* **Capability 4:** Documents regulatory constraints (GDPR, HIPAA, FCC, safety standards).

## ⚠️ Anti-Patterns & Warnings

* **Anti-Pattern:** Setting non-measurable KPIs without clear verification methods.
* **Anti-Pattern:** Ignoring unit economics and monthly cloud infrastructure budgets.
* **Anti-Pattern:** Neglecting compliance and regulatory standards during early planning.
