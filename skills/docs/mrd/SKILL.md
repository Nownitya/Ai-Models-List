# Market Requirements Document (MRD) Skill

Standardized engineering skill for creating, updating, and verifying **MRD** documentation conforming to Layer 15 Universal Project Documentation Standard.

## When to Use

* Evaluating market feasibility and competitive landscape for new products.
* Identifying market gaps and competitor deficiencies.
* Defining value proposition and market segment sizing.

---

## 🎯 Core Capabilities

* **Capability 1:** Synthesizes market problem statements and total addressable market (TAM).
* **Capability 2:** Creates competitive positioning matrices comparing key features and pricing.
* **Capability 3:** Defines customer personas and adoption barriers.

## ⚠️ Anti-Patterns & Warnings

* **Anti-Pattern:** Assuming competitor capabilities without factual market evidence.
* **Anti-Pattern:** Over-estimating market sizing without segmentation.
