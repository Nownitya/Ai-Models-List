---

## 1. Business Context & Strategic Alignment

### 1.1 Commercial Objectives
1. **Zero-Hardware-Cost Disruption:** Eliminate the $400/rider entry barrier for off-grid group communications by repurposing commodity smartphones into professional-grade mesh communicators.
2. **Democratize Cross-Platform Intercom:** End the Cardo vs Sena proprietary walled garden by establishing an open, cross-platform standard that connects Android and iOS riders seamlessly.
3. **Zero Infrastructure Operating Costs:** Maintain core local BLE mesh functionality with $0 hosting expense, zero database subscriptions, and zero cloud lock-in for recreational convoys.
4. **Dual Commercial Funnel:** Leverage viral organic B2C adoption to seed high-value B2B fleet enterprise subscriptions across tour agencies, rally organizers, and search & rescue teams.

## 2. Stakeholder Matrix & Impact Analysis

* **Motorcycle Convoy Leaders:** Demand zero-friction pairing, offline topographic contour visibility, and real-time altitude separation over trail passes.
* **Casual Weekend Riders:** Price-sensitive; want to talk to friends without buying a $400 helmet gadget.
* **Commercial Tour Operators (B2B):** Require real-time convoy tracking, multi-group radio channel partitioning, and rugged stationary basecamp repeaters.
* **Search & Rescue (SAR) Units:** Require peer-to-peer off-grid reliability, zero single points of failure, and satellite gateway lifeline compatibility.
